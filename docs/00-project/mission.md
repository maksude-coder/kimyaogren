# Mission Statement

## Purpose

Defines the fundamental reason KimyaÖğren exists. This is the anchor for all strategic decisions.

---

## Mission

> **Empower every Turkish high school student to master chemistry through curriculum-aligned, scientifically-proven learning methods — making YKS success achievable for all, regardless of background.**

---

## Mission Deconstruction

| Element | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Every Turkish high school student** | Inclusive, nationwide, grades 9-12 |
| **Master chemistry** | Deep understanding, not memorization |
| **Curriculum-aligned** | 100% MEB (Ministry of Education) compliant |
| **Scientifically-proven learning methods** | Spaced repetition, active recall, mastery-based progression |
| **YKS success achievable for all** | Primary measurable outcome |
| **Regardless of background** | Equity: urban/rural, public/private, socioeconomic |

---

## Scope Boundaries

### In Scope
- Chemistry curriculum for grades 9-12 (MEB)
- YKS (TYT/AYT) chemistry preparation
- Student self-study + teacher classroom support
- Turkish language, Turkey market

### Out of Scope
- Other subjects (Physics, Biology, Math) — future epics
- University-level chemistry
- International curricula (IB, AP, A-Level)
- Social features, gamification, video streaming

---

## Strategic Pillars

| Pillar | Description | KPI |
|--------|-------------|-----|
| **Curriculum Fidelity** | Every topic maps to MEB learning objectives | 100% coverage |
| **Learning Science** | Spaced repetition, mastery-based progression | Retention > 70% |
| **Teacher Partnership** | Tools that amplify, not replace, teachers | Teacher NPS > 50 |
| **Equitable Access** | Mobile-first, offline-capable, low-bandwidth | Rural adoption > 30% |
| **Data-Driven Iteration** | Continuous improvement from learning analytics | Quarterly content updates |

---

## Mission-Driven Decision Framework

When facing trade-offs, prioritize in this order:

1. **Student Learning Outcomes** — Does it improve mastery?
2. **Curriculum Alignment** — Does it match MEB/YKS?
3. **Teacher Usability** — Does it fit classroom workflow?
4. **Technical Excellence** — Is it maintainable?
5. **Cost Efficiency** — Does it justify investment?
